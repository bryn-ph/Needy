# Changelog

## v0.5.0-beta

Needs a full game restart, not just /reload.

- Shared wishlists. See who in your group or guild wants the same items (they need Needy too).
  - DungeonJournal loot rows show how many others want each item. Hover for names.
  - Wishlist tab has Mine and Everyone's tabs. Orange means someone in your group wants it too.
  - Untick Include guildmates to only count your group.
  - Only item IDs are shared. Turn it off with Share my wishlist.
- New look for the Wishlist tab. Settings window is bigger.
- Wishlist mark is now a heart.
- Minimap button opens the Wishlist tab.
- Removed most tooltips and descriptions from the settings.
- /needy share shows sharing status. /needy test share adds fake wishlists to try it out.

## v0.4.0-beta

Needs a full game restart.

- Themes for the roll bars and Roll Log: Original (default), Blizzard, Modern and Minimal.
- The timer stays on the bar after a roll ends instead of disappearing.
- Fixed: wiping ended the run. Releasing and running back in now keeps the same run. Taking the spirit healer ends it after 5 minutes outside.
- Settings reorganised, with a Test rolls button at the top.

## v0.3.1-beta

- The roll animation only plays for your own roll. If you passed, the bar just says You passed.

## v0.3.0-beta

Needs a full game restart.

- Optional glow around new roll bars (off by default).
- Fixed: the result sometimes showed the winner's roll instead of yours.

## v0.2.0-beta

Needs a full game restart.

- Run Stats tab on the end of run report, including luck (items won vs. even odds).
- Wishlist, if you have DungeonJournal. Right-click an item there to add it. Its roll bar lights up and plays a sound when it drops.
- Removed posting awards to chat.

## v0.1.0-beta

First beta.

- Roll bars that replace Blizzard's need/greed/pass popups.
- Roll Log during a run, and history of past runs.
- Joke titles at the end of a run.
- Test mode: /needy test and /needy test run.
