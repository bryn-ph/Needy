# Needy

Loot roll addon for WoW: Forever. It replaces the need/greed/pass popups, keeps a log of every roll in a run, and gives out joke titles at the end.

Beta. Report bugs on [GitHub](https://github.com/bryn-ph/Needy/issues).

## Features

- Roll bars instead of Blizzard's popups. Shows who picked what, then the winner and their roll.
- Four themes: Original, Blizzard, Modern and Minimal.
- Roll Log during a run, and a history of past runs.
- Titles and stats at the end of a run.
- Wishlist (needs DungeonJournal). Items on it light up when they drop.
- Shared wishlists. See who in your group or guild wants the same items.
- Test mode, to try it outside a dungeon.

## Usage

- Minimap button: left-click for the wishlist and settings, right-click for roll history.
- Right-click an item in DungeonJournal to add it to your wishlist.
- `/needy test` for practice rolls, `/needy help` for all commands.
