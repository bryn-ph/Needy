# Needy

A loot roll overhaul for **WoW: Forever**. Needy replaces the need/greed/pass popups with cleaner roll bars, keeps a record of every roll in the dungeon or raid, and hands out (mostly unflattering) titles when the run is over.

> **Beta.** Please [report issues here](https://github.com/bryn-ph/Needy/issues).

## Features

- Roll bars in place of Blizzard's popups, showing who has chosen what as it happens, then the winner and their roll.
- A Roll Log button on screen during a run: click it for everything that's dropped, who won it and with what roll.
- Roll History for the current and past runs.
- Run Awards when you leave the instance, for whoever stood out.
- Test mode, so you can try it all outside an instance.

## Usage

Needy works on its own: the next time something drops in a group, the roll bars appear.

- Click the minimap button for settings (also under *Options → AddOns → Needy*); right-click it for the roll history.
- To move the roll bars, untick *Lock position* in settings and drag the handle. It snaps to the centre of the screen.
- `/needy test` shows test rolls with a fake party. `/needy help` lists the other commands.
